# Market Observer – Knowledge System

Market Observer is an expert system designed to study financial markets.

The system does not trade.

Its purpose is to accumulate structured knowledge about market behavior and recommendation performance.

## Knowledge Layers

Layer 1: Market Data
Layer 2: Observations
Layer 3: Recommendations
Layer 4: Execution Simulation
Layer 5: Performance Review
Layer 6: Strategy Evaluation

## Learning Principle

MO does not "learn" through black-box AI models.

Instead it learns through:
- structured observation
- historical validation
- statistical evaluation

This ensures transparency and reproducibility.

## Final Principle

Market Observer is a knowledge engine.

The system's value comes from accumulated understanding of market behavior over time.
